# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Everyday adults who want practical personal finance guidance in plain English. They may be anxious, avoidant, skeptical of finance jargon, or simply trying to make a better decision without becoming a hobbyist. They use the handbook as a calm reference when a money question becomes relevant in real life.

## Product Purpose

Financial Handbook explains personal finance topics with clarity, directness, and low judgment. Success means readers understand the decision in front of them, know what to do next, and feel less intimidated by money conversations, tradeoffs, and paperwork.

## Brand Personality

Calm, candid, practical. The voice should feel like a financially literate friend who respects the reader enough to be direct, but never talks down to them.

## Anti-references

Avoid finance-bro dashboards, dense institutional paperwork, generic SaaS card walls, overdesigned bento layouts, decorative sticky sidebars, and therapy-adjacent prompts that make practical guidance feel like a coaching product. Do not turn long-form handbook pages into landing pages.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve reading flow. The article is the primary interface.
2. Reduce intimidation. Dense topics should feel approachable without being diluted.
3. Make scanning easier without dashboarding the content.
4. Use restraint over novelty. Visual emphasis should clarify hierarchy, not compete with it.
5. Keep patterns consistent across topic pages unless the content genuinely earns an exception.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Respect reduced motion. Keep line lengths readable, preserve semantic heading order, and never rely on color alone to communicate meaning.
